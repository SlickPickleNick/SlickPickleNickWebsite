Building Interactive, Community-Driven Streams

I create gaming and community streams designed around active chat participation, custom stream tools, and viewer challenges. Rather than just broadcasting gameplay, I build systems where viewers can directly trigger in-game challenges, play chat mini-games, queue music, and shape live moments.

Outside of content creation, I am a graduate student completing my master’s degree in Spring 2027. My academic and professional background centers around live experiences, event operations, hospitality, guest experience, and community engagement.

That background directly shapes my approach to streaming. Every broadcast is organized like a digital live event — with engaging pacing, thoughtful hospitality for new viewers, and interactive systems that turn passive viewers into active participants.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do you usually stream?
Streams are announced in advance in the official Discord server. Because of graduate school schedules, exact start times are posted weekly in the #announcements channel.
How do song requests work?
You can request songs in Twitch chat using !sr [Song and Artist]. To check your song queue position, use !myRequests, or view what is currently playing with !song.
What is the GeoGuessr Torch Game?
The Torch Game is an interactive chat mini-game during GeoGuessr streams. Viewers join the pool by typing !torch. When selected, the torch bearer can type !stop to freeze Nick's movement and force an instant guess.
Can I suggest new Channel Point rewards or commands?
Yes! Head over to the official Discord server and post your idea in the suggestions channel. Many of our best stream rewards came directly from community members.
Where can I buy official merch?
You can browse topographic desk mats and official creator apparel in the Official Merch Shop.
